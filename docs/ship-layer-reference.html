Bubble ship — geometry and style review

The working reference for the ship's geometry, art and playable spaces. We build and review one level at a time, opening areas as they become playable.

Current build: open Commons arcade​

The bridge lift reaches an open civic plaza and a curved, planted arcade in the approved building band. This replaces the generic barrel-roofed hall. Cream terraces, teal ribs, open bays and civic seating begin translating the approved concept into measured, playable geometry. Other zones and tiers remain coarse.

Drag to orbit · Scroll or pinch to zoom. Model orbit is diagnostic; screenshots below use physical player positions.

Commons dimensions and assets

The first arcade occupies radii 43–55 m in the approved outer building band, linked to the 22 × 30 m plaza by a guarded walk. Scale figures are 1.7 m. Geometry is checked against the 95 m inner envelope. Painted modules, separate collision and walking data, and simpler distant geometry derive from the same Blender master.

Approved concept and zoning reference

The approved direction is sweeping terraces, open arcades, planting and civic life. The zoning sets an open plaza, curved arcade groups and planted walks; exact details develop through playable sections. The earlier paintover is a finish study only.

Decisions​

Still to review​

  • Commons: review the first arcade/plaza section against the approved concept before expanding.
  • Later tiers: concepts and zoning remain proposals; population and life-support capacity are unresolved.
  • Laptop performance: actual M2 Air measurement is pending. The latest Studio run is about 9.6 ms p95 at 1920×1080.
  • Travel effects: physically supported glow/bubble effects remain future work. The current demo does not show travel glow or GR lensing.
  • Solar System flyby: planet textures exist; a moving flyby with suitable playback pacing is still to do.

Play the latest Mac review​
