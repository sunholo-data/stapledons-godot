# Restored physical bridge floor

The original bridge stores its flat visible top in `WALK_bridge_deck`. The early seven-tier visual generator discarded `WALK_` objects; this removed the actual top while retaining the tapered deck underside. At the captain spawn, that underside is 81.4283 m rather than the approved 82 m floor height. Lower shadow bias cannot close that physical gap.

`bridge_floor_top82` restores only the original top triangles as a separate opaque render mesh. It retains the original cream/ochre/teal/coral material regions, reusing the existing ash-floor and packed bridge pigments with continuous ship XY metre UVs at the established four-metre repeat. The real lift opening at ship XY 6–10 m is clipped out. It adds 4,217 visual triangles, with no new materials or texture images: the base export is 153 mesh instances / 200,399 triangles, 14 materials and 10 images. The unified export includes the same surface and the existing Commons assembly.

Both WALK files are byte-identical. Captain position, prop geometry and bases, the 82 m tier datum, dimensions, sphere, lift, collision surfaces, camera and lighting are unchanged. This is a geometric contact fix, not a shadow decal or a physics change. Every existing mesh's coordinates, topology, UVs and material assignments have identical before/after digests in the provenance JSONs; the only new visual object is the restored top.

Distinct editable masters are `stapledon_bridge_mesh_grounded_v2.blend` and `stapledon_unified_ship_grounded_v2.blend`. Both retain packed pigments and the restoration generator as a text datablock. Canonical authoring uses `/Users/voightkampff/dev/blender/scripts/stapledon_bridge_floor_restore_v2.py` through that workspace's `./blender.sh`, based on authoring commit `e4da963`. Exact script and asset hashes are in `asset-pins.json`. Run the restoration after the historical v1 material pass; never rerun the historical visual generator alone, as it removes the original floor again. No Python asset pipeline is added to the game.

The new measured floor test first failed eight support/material assertions against the prior export. The restored export passes 14 checks: actual visible triangle intersections at four player positions are 82 m within 1 mm, original material regions match, the shaft is open at three points and both WALK SHA-256 hashes match. The seven-tier geometry/shaft validator also passes. Both masters pass Blender scene inspection without missing external files, and both GLBs pass clean reimport. Logs are in `validation/`. Native contact/shadow review is performed separately by the integration owner; this folder does not claim new GPU captures or final shadow tuning.
