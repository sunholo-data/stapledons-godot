## Verification and limitations

All nine files were decoded and verified as native 1024 × 1536, 8-bit RGBA PNGs, with real zero-alpha background pixels. The selected images were visually inspected. They contain one person each, with no chair, desk, console, text, or scenery.

The person silhouettes meet the 40-pixel inset after the front-console second candidates (measuring alpha >=128). However, the generated alpha channels contain sparse stray pixels outside the main silhouettes, including within the border. Thus the strict requirement of alpha zero at *every* pixel outside the person is not fully met. Generated alpha was preserved rather than silently cleaned by another image-processing method. The original preserved frame also has stray alpha.

Guide registration is approximate, not pixel-exact. In particular, the rear seated/near-seated heads landed higher than the numerical guide target, and boot positions/spacing vary between frames. The seated rear body is visibly lowered relative to standing, with spread coat and bent legs; no claim is made that its height is exactly 65% or that the sequence is production-registered without further adjustment. The front console pair has improved edge clearance, but its head and hand positions still vary. Rear-view forearms are substantially occluded/foreshortened; palms face down rather than forming a hands-up gesture.
