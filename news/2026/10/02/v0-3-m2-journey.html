v0.3: plan, commit, transit, arrive

October 2, 2026
Sunholo
Stapledon's Voyage team

The journey core is complete: pick a star, commit, watch both clocks run, and arrive. Once you commit, there is no going back.

What's new​

  • Commit: hold for 1.5 s. After that the simulation refuses every cancel, and says so.
  • Transit and arrival: the phase, both clocks, the distance flown and the energy radiated, live, until you arrive.
  • Deterministic replay: a 10,000-tick session replays byte for byte on the AILANG VM and the interpreter.
  • v0.3.1 opens straight onto the galaxy map; v0.3.2 adds trackpad zoom and Retina scaling.

Not yet​

The interior of the ship, and a journey you live through on board, are the next milestone (M4).

Releases: v0.3.0, v0.3.1, v0.3.2 (macOS review builds)
