The wall is absolute for anything with mass. No dust, no gas, no gift from a
stranger, no crew member stepping outside. What you brought is what you have.

Light crosses freely, in both directions, at every colour and energy. That is
the one part of the wall that real physics already allows, because light does
not feel the Higgs field. Neutrinos cross too. They pass through planets as
easily as through the hull, and the wall lets them be.

There was once a belief aboard that a trickle of hydrogen leaked in from
space. It does not, and it must not. Near the speed of light, the thin gas
between the stars arrives as a beam. At 0.999999c every proton would strike
with an energy of 663.5 GeV, more than many particle accelerators reach. A
leaking wall would be a weapon pointed at the bow.

So the ledger is closed. Births, meals, repairs and new machines are all made
from mass already aboard. Nothing material leaves either, only light: the
light the drive emits, and the faint light of the boundary glow.

You carry ideas, not cargo. Everything you learn from the galaxy arrives as
light.
