# M4.4 S4 copy review: news from home, labels and reason phrases

Pause point S4 (Mark approves the news template copy before M4.4 merges). Everything below is
verbatim from `data/news/templates.json` and `data/news/copy.json`; nothing else on the news panel
or the legacy screen is player-facing text. Approve in one read, or mark lines to change; the
copy is data, so a change needs no code. `make news-lint` (in `make test`) holds the rules.

## Rules the copy obeys (enforced)

- Numerals appear ONLY inside a `{slot}`; each slot is one sim field formatted `%.2f` (D-21, D-12).
  Slots: `{elapsed_years}` (Earth years since departure at the news epoch), `{news_age_years}`
  (how old the message is when it arrives), `{news_epoch}` (allowed, unused so far).
- At most 280 characters once filled (checked at a worst-case slot width), five tiers of four
  templates (the sim draws the variant), facts only (no judgement of the player's choices).
- The header is built from fields, not from a template: "Latest news from Earth: Earth-year
  +0.04, already 4.35 years old."

## Fixed labels (shown through DisplayBinding; digit-free)

- `title`: "Transmission received"
- `header_lead`: "Latest news from Earth: Earth-year "

- `ai_tag`: "generated"

- `diagnostics_title`: "Diagnostics"
- `diagnostics_empty`: "No transmission problems."
- `diagnostics_entry`: "News fell back to the archived text; reason code"
- `legacy_title`: "Legacy"
- `legacy_away`: "You were away "
- `legacy_older`: " years. Home is "
- `legacy_tail`: " years older."
- `begin_again`: "Begin again"
- `return_hint`: "Sol is highlighted. Plan the voyage home and hold to commit."

The three provenance renderings of the news tab:

- `template` (no request made: AI off, no key, text-only): no tag, no notice.
- `ai`: the accepted text, with the small tag "generated".
- `fallback`: the template paragraph under the notice "Live transmission unavailable: archived text shown", then "Reason: <phrase>", and one line in the diagnostics list: "News fell back to the archived text; reason code <code> (<phrase>)".

## Reason code -> phrase table (digit-free; an unknown code is an error, never blank)

| sim reason code | phrase shown after "Reason:" |
|---|---|
| `offline` | the live service was switched off |
| `no_key` | no service key was available |
| `text_only` | text-only mode does not allow this request |
| `timeout` | the service took too long to answer |
| `service_down` | the service was not running |
| `budget` | the spending limit was reached |
| `provider_error` | the provider returned an error |
| `expired` | no reply in time |
| `ai_hash` | the generated text arrived damaged |
| `ai_length` | the generated text was too long |
| `ai_numeral` | the generated text contained a numeral |
| `ai_markup` | the generated text was badly formed |
| `ai_emotion` | the generated text used a feeling that is not allowed |
| `ai_descriptor` | the generated description was not valid |
| `ai_kind` | the reply was not the kind of text asked for |

## Template paragraphs by tier (tone set by the tier; slots in braces)

### Tier 0 (under 1 Earth-year: family news)

1. Your family sent word within days of your leaving. Everyone is well and the house is quiet. Only {elapsed_years} years have passed there since you left, and the message is already {news_age_years} years old.
2. A short message from home: the family gathered to watch you go and are all in good health. They have started a list of things to tell you. At home, {elapsed_years} years have gone by since you left.
3. Home writes that nothing has changed yet. The kitchen is as you left it, and your family keeps your place at the table. This news is {news_age_years} years old; {elapsed_years} years have passed on Earth since your departure.
4. Your family sends greetings. The first weeks without you were hard but ordinary, and they are well. {elapsed_years} years of Earth time lie between your departure and the day this message was sent.

### Tier 1 (1-5: elections, a niece starting school)

1. Earth sends word after {elapsed_years} years. There has been an election and the new government has taken office. A niece has started school. The news is {news_age_years} years old.
2. Home reports {elapsed_years} years of ordinary change: a new head of the space agency, a town fair revived, and a niece learning to read. This message is {news_age_years} years old.
3. After {elapsed_years} years on Earth, the family writes of small changes: a house repainted, a new school year begun, a vote held and counted. The transmission is {news_age_years} years old.
4. A letter from Earth, {elapsed_years} years after your departure: prices have shifted, a children's choir has formed in the old neighbourhood, and the family still talks about you. It left home {news_age_years} years ago.

### Tier 2 (5-15: a parent's illness or death, a new technology)

1. Earth writes after {elapsed_years} years. An older relative has died, and the family marked the day without you. A new kind of battery now powers the cities. The message is {news_age_years} years old.
2. Much has changed in {elapsed_years} years. A parent fell ill and has since been laid to rest. New machines do work that people once did by hand. This news is {news_age_years} years old.
3. In the {elapsed_years} years since you left, home has grown older. Friends have moved away, a parent has grown frail, and a new communications network now covers the planet. The message is {news_age_years} years old.
4. Earth reports {elapsed_years} years of change: a new technology in every pocket, a sibling's children almost grown, and a parent's long illness. This transmission is {news_age_years} years old.

### Tier 3 (15-50: a generation turned over)

1. {elapsed_years} years have passed on Earth. The children you knew are adults with children of their own, and the old offices are held by people who never met you. The message is {news_age_years} years old.
2. A generation has turned over at home in {elapsed_years} years. Your friends are old or gone, and their grandchildren run the schools. Your voyage is now a family story. The message is {news_age_years} years old.
3. After {elapsed_years} years, Earth is run by people born after you left. The speech of your childhood has shifted, and your name is kept in a family archive. This message is {news_age_years} years old.
4. Earth sends word across {elapsed_years} years. Those who saw you leave are very old or have died; their descendants wrote this. The message is {news_age_years} years old.

### Tier 4 (50 and over: names nobody remembers)

1. {elapsed_years} years have passed on Earth. The names of the people who saw you leave are known only to historians. Whoever wrote this found your voyage in an old record. The message is {news_age_years} years old.
2. Home answers after {elapsed_years} years, in a language that has drifted from the one you spoke. No one now living remembers your departure. The message is {news_age_years} years old.
3. Across {elapsed_years} years, Earth has been remade. The cities, the nations and the names you knew belong to history books. The message is {news_age_years} years old.
4. Whoever keeps the archive at home has found your name after {elapsed_years} years. The people you left are only names in it. This message is {news_age_years} years old.

## Items for Mark

1. **Canon check (D-42, D-43, D-44, 2026-10-06 postdates this plan).** The copy is written to the
   approved M4.4 plan and has NOT been redesigned around the new canon. Lines that may now be
   wrong, for you to rule on: every tier speaks of "your family" or a parent, a niece, a sibling
   (tiers 0-2), which assumes the player left a family at home; D-42 makes the player a
   returning pilot, so "family" may need to become "the people who sent you" or a crew-neutral
   voice. Tier 4 talks of an archive that "found your name", which may collide with the spire reset
   (D-43). The legacy screen's closing line ("You were away ... Home is ... years older.") and the
   "Begin again" label (new voyage, new seed, no reload) read fine against D-44 (a captain's death
   resets the run) but have not been checked against the spire-reset wording.
2. **At Sol the message is 0.00 years old.** The light-time at home is zero, so on the return the
   header reads "already 0.00 years old" and the `{news_age_years}` sentences read oddly
   ("This transmission is 0.00 years old"). Options: leave it (it is true), or give tiers a
   no-age variant for the home arrival. Not changed here: it needs a copy decision.
3. **Tier 0 sample values.** Real sim values at alpha Cen: Earth-year +0.04, age 4.31 years.
4. The reason phrases for `ai_descriptor` and `ai_kind` cannot occur for news text today; they
   exist so a future media path never meets an unknown code.
