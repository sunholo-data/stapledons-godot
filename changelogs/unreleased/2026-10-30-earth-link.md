### Added
- **Earth's link** (R3-EARTH-LINK; R3 S1, K1, F6). Earth's signal now obeys light travel: the ship sends its plan home at every commit, Earth beams only at plans it has heard, and the ship is in Earth's beam or not by geometry alone. The live Earth rate (how fast home's history arrives) follows speed and heading: near still flying away near c, real time at rest, a torrent heading home. The light-cone maths is the new `sunholo/observe` 0.1.0 package (protocol 2.16).
- **The comms station** on the bridge, visual first: Earth's feed (home's weeks as pictures, slowed, sped or jumbled by the Earth rate; the last week under static out of beam, with a ring until Earth's voice returns), Earth's clock beside the ship's, the crew's faces at their home feelings, and the exchange drawn as light. Live frames are painted each time you look, with your own keys; the library builds up. The words are one tap away (Details).
- **Sound from home** at the comms station: synthesised beds for each kind of week (family, home, town, world, science, sky), the torrent's babble and a receiver hiss out of beam, slowed and deepened or sped up by the Earth rate.
- **Earth's beams on the galaxy map** (on by default): the beam Earth aims at the plan it has heard, your plans on their way home, beams left aimed at ports the ship has gone from, the wait at the port for Earth's light, and, while you plan, the wait the new leg would cost.
