### Infrastructure
- **The nightly suite runs in two halves** (`tools/rig_nightly.sh`). `make test` (headless) posts `rig/full` whenever the CPU is free; `make golden` posts `rig/golden` once the GPU is idle too. Gating both on an idle GPU let the overnight local-model evals (88–100 % GPU from 22:30 to 06:15) block every run after 2026-11-04, so main went unchecked for two nights. `tools/rig_check.sh --golden-only` runs the GPU half alone. The `nightly-rig` issue closes only when neither half is red on the commit.

### Fixed
- **The sound test's S2 beacon checks no longer depend on wall time** (#392). Under background QoS, as for a launchd job, the headless Dummy audio driver mixes slower than the clock: a 6.2 s wait captured under 4 s of audio (one pip at D 0.5), and a whole-window crossing count misread the 28.4 Hz True carrier by up to 1.5 % against a 1 % check. The capture now waits for its frames of audio, and the carrier is measured from sub-sample zero crossings that skip the keying's silences, with a negative control (a keyed 28.4 Hz sine reads 28.400 Hz; 2× and 0.5× do not pass). The release-fade check is taken before a frame passes, since a throttled frame can outlast the 80 ms fade. Reproduced with `taskpolicy -b make sound-test` (old: 3 failures; new: 83 passed, 0 failures).
- **The nightly launchd job runs as `ProcessType Interactive`**, so macOS does not throttle it like a background job (the cause of #392's slow mixing). **Merges never wait on the GPU** (CLAUDE.md, Mark 2026-11-06): targeted gates are headless; `make golden` belongs to the nightly `rig/golden` and Mark's dev build.
