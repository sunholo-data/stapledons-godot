### Added
- **The Doppler indicator at the comms station** (R3-TRUE-PROCESSED-b, Mark 2026-11-03). In the station's Receiver (Processed) setting a soft gradient bleeds in from the feed screen's edge, blue as the ship closes on Earth and red as it leaves, strongest at the very edge and gone a tenth of the way in, with no border, labelled "Doppler ×D · shown" with the live Earth rate. The colour is a reference line at 555 nm seen at 555 / D (clamped to the indicator's limits, 400 to 650 nm), the strength |ln D| / ln 1414.2 (nothing at rest, full at the top speed). The picture underneath runs to the edge exactly as decoded, and the True setting shows no indicator at all. `make capture-doppler-indicator` renders the review frames; the GPU golden checks the gradient at RS-26 and RS-29.
