### Added
- **The Doppler indicator at the comms station** (R3-TRUE-PROCESSED-b, Mark 2026-11-03). In the station's Receiver (Processed) setting the feed screen's bezel glows blue as the ship closes on Earth and red as it leaves, labelled "Doppler ×D · shown" with the live Earth rate. The colour is a reference line at 555 nm seen at 555 / D (clamped to the eye's band), the strength |ln D| / ln 1414.2 (nothing at rest, full at the top speed). The picture itself stays exactly as decoded and the True setting shows no indicator. `make capture-doppler-indicator` renders the review frames; the GPU golden checks the bezel at RS-26 and RS-29.
