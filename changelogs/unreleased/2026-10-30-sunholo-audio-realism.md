### Changed
- **Sound from home, closer to real** (R3-SUNHOLO-AUDIO-REALISM). The comms station's home sounds are re-voiced with the new `sunholo/audio` 0.6.0 tools (resonant formant filters, pink and brown noise, FM bells, a plucked string, events inside loops, a short reverb): talkers you can't make out instead of hiss, wind and leaves, steps and a passing car, a far crowd, real cricket chirps, a music-box tune, bells in the square's echo. Every other sound is byte for byte unchanged.
- **The station's sound is exact** (standing order: no compromises on the physics). Home plays at the true Earth rate: in slow motion flying away, fast-forward turning for home. **Receiver** (the default) corrects the pitch as a real receiver would, so home keeps its true voice; **True** plays the raw shifted sound, which near light speed falls below or above human hearing, and the station says so. A button on the feed switches between them.
