### Added

- The universe lab's scenario files: a scenario is a small named, versioned file that states every setting of a simulated universe, and each setting that departs from the published literature must say why and where the value comes from, or the lab refuses to run it. Three first scenarios: the literature baseline, pure physics, and Lonely, the fixed median universe (R3-UNIVERSE-LAB UL3a, `sim/lab_scenario.ail`, `data/lab/scenarios/`, `make lab-test`).
