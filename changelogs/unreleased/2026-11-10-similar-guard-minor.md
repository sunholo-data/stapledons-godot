### Fixed
- **`make similar-test` is green again.** Its main.gd guard checked for the literal name `SimBridge.EARTH_MINOR`, which PR #379 renamed to `EARTH_LOG_MINOR`, so the suite failed on its last line after every ranking and session step had passed. The guard now reads the property instead: the map session's `want_minor` must be one bare `SimBridge.<NAME>_MINOR`, and that constant must be at or above `SIMILAR_MINOR` in `bridge/sim_bridge.gd`. Protocol bumps pass; a downgrade or a rewritten assignment fails. Anything the grammar does not recognise (an expression, a ternary, a second mention of `want_minor`) fails closed.
- **The guard has its own controls.** `make similar-guard-controls` (`tools/similar_guard_controls.sh`) runs 16 mutated copies of main.gd and the constants: 13 must be rejected and 3 accepted (the pristine tree, a future protocol bump and a trailing comment). A control that does not go red fails the target.
- **The guard runs in `ci-fast`.** `similar-test` and `ci-fast` both depend on `similar-main-guard` and `similar-guard-controls`, so the fast tier now catches a drift that #379 merged past.
