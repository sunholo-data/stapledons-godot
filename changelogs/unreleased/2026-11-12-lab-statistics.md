### Added

- The universe lab's statistics: averages with honest error bars, exact confidence intervals for the median and the 10th and 90th percentiles, and the rule that says whether a target is met, missed or still unsettled. Results run in batches of 64 seeds up to 1,024, with a check after every batch, and two scenarios can be compared seed by seed. Because checking sixteen times gives sixteen chances to be wrong, each check uses a stricter level, so that over a whole run a target is wrongly called met, or wrongly called missed, at most 2.5 % of the time each; a plain 95 % interval is kept for reporting a final figure. A band includes its ends, so a count that is always exactly 5 meets "5 to 10". Every number is checked against an independent reference written in a second language, which also simulates the whole rule 10,000 times (R3-UNIVERSE-LAB UL3c, `sim/lab_stats.ail`, `tools/lab_stats_ref.py`, `make lab-test`). Nothing in the game uses it yet.
