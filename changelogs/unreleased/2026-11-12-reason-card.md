### The reason card (R3-REASONS RT5a)

- A new card shows why a world might be worth the journey: the spectrum the ship recorded, the target through the telescope at its real sharpness, how sure the reading is and how old the light, which of the ship's eight resources that kind of world could improve, and what going costs in Earth years, ship years and mass. It runs on an invented example fixture for now and is not yet on the map or the Navigation console.
