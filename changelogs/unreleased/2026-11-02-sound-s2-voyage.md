### Added
- **The voyage is heard** (R2-SOUND S2). Plotting a course gives a soft tick from the navigation computer; holding to commit plays a tone that rises with the hold and stops if you let go; the sim's acceptance seals it with the low chord; the drive rises, falls silent for the coast and brakes; the dust counter at the helm ticks for each grain that flashes on the dome (with a Geiger counter's 25 ms dead time); arrival gets its quiet chord.
- **The home beacon** at the helm: Earth's carrier, keyed once a second of Earth's time, heard while Earth's beam reaches the ship. It runs at the true Earth rate (no compression): flying away at 0.99c Earth's pips come 14 s apart, turning for home 14 a second. **Receiver** (the comms station's default) keeps the carrier at its true 400 Hz; **True** plays it as it arrives, down at 28 Hz outbound or up at 5.6 kHz homebound, and silent beyond human hearing. It follows the station's True / Receiver button.
