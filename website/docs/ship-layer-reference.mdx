## Decisions

| Settled | Direction |
|---|---|
| Ship geometry | Seven major tiers in a 200 m bubble; lower architecture follows a 95 m inner envelope for at least 5 m clearance. |
| Bridge | Keep the actual 44 m deck at +82 m and needle tip at +98 m. |
| Viewpoint | Ship and sky share one observer. Floors and roofs remain opaque. |
| Asset workflow | One measured Blender assembly, reusable painted modules and simpler distant models. |
| Release approach | Develop the Commons first, then open further levels incrementally. |
| Commons layout | Approved zoning direction: open civic plaza, curved arcades and planted walks; build and review one section at a time. |
| Travel sky | Use 4× exposure for now, preserving physical inputs and directional contrast. It remains a labelled display aid. |
| Star inspection | Hold I and click a known, visible star for catalogue facts; open that same star in the map explicitly. |

## Still to review

- **Commons:** review the first arcade/plaza section against the approved concept before expanding.
- **Later tiers:** concepts and zoning remain proposals; population and life-support capacity are unresolved.
- **Bridge finish:** review this first texture pass before refining the ink detail.
- **Laptop performance:** the M2 Air baseline is roughly 60 FPS at rest, with occasional stalls and 18–19 ms p95 in most ship views. The new build still needs a laptop comparison; Studio timings do not establish Air performance.
- **Travel effects:** the forward wall glow now uses the package’s greybody spectrum with ε = 1e-10. Other bubble effects and GR lensing remain future work; external sunlight on the ship is still unfinished.
- **Solar departure tour:** Earth → Sun → Jupiter → Callisto → Saturn → Alpha Centauri system → Alpha Centauri A at 1 AU. The guided session uses 1 g and slower approach playback. Close stops turn the ship smoothly to place the body beside the bridge. Callisto provides collision-safe clearance before Saturn. These are inertial stops, not gravity-bound orbits; thermal hazards and GR are not modelled. Earth uses the Earth–Moon barycentre approximation.

## Play the latest Mac review

With GitHub CLI and Google Cloud CLI authenticated:

```sh
gh api repos/sunholo-data/stapledons-godot/contents/tools/install_review_build.sh --jq .content | base64 -d | bash -s -- --dev
```

The app opens the title menu. Choose **Board the ship** to begin at rest near Earth.
**Mouse movement** looks around by default and **WASD** walks. **Tab** opens help,
details and **Walk to** buttons, releasing the pointer for interaction. Consoles,
inspection and arrival panels also own the pointer; **Esc** closes the top panel.

Use **Tab → Walk to → Navigation station**, then **E**, to plan and commit.
**M** opens a read-only chart. Hold **I** to identify and click visible stars or bodies.
**P** pauses or resumes time while preserving the course. **7/8/9** look
forward/side/aft, and **J** adjusts sky brightness. The **Voyage console** starts
the guided voyage; the **Archive terminal** holds physics entries and news.

The chart shows local clouds by default. **Interstellar medium [D]** toggles them,
**Local ISM** frames the nearby clouds, and **Depth guides** adds optional radial
construction lines. Approximate depths are labelled in the legend.

Developer launches retain frozen snapshots and the benchmark; packaged menu
launches expose the player controls. [Try it](/docs/try-it) distinguishes the latest
authenticated review from the earlier public download.
