## Current build: one playable ship

The bridge lift reaches an open civic plaza and a curved, planted arcade in the
approved building band. This replaces the generic barrel-roofed hall. Cream terraces,
teal ribs, open bays and civic seating begin translating the approved concept into
measured, playable geometry. Other zones and tiers remain coarse.

## Decisions

| Settled | Direction |
|---|---|
| Ship geometry | Seven major tiers in a 200 m bubble; lower architecture follows a 95 m inner envelope for at least 5 m clearance. |
| Bridge | Keep the actual 44 m deck at +82 m and needle tip at +98 m. |
| Viewpoint | Ship and sky share one observer. Floors and roofs remain opaque. |
| Asset workflow | One measured Blender assembly, reusable painted modules and simpler distant models. |
| Release approach | Develop the Commons first, then open further levels incrementally. |
| Commons layout | Approved zoning direction: open civic plaza, curved arcades and planted walks; build and review one section at a time. |
| Travel sky | Use 4× exposure for now, preserving physical inputs and directional contrast. It remains a labelled display aid. |
| Star inspection | Hold I and click a known, visible star for catalogue facts; open that same star in the map explicitly. |

## Still to review

- **Commons:** review the first arcade/plaza section against the approved concept before expanding.
- **Later tiers:** concepts and zoning remain proposals; population and life-support capacity are unresolved.
- **Bridge finish:** review this first texture pass before refining the ink detail.
- **Laptop performance:** actual M2 Air measurement is pending. The latest Studio test measured 9.3–9.7 ms p95 at 1920×1080; star inspection adds CPU work despite little frame-time change on that machine.
- **Travel effects:** physically supported glow/bubble effects remain future work. The current demo does not show travel glow or GR lensing.
- **Solar System flyby:** planet textures exist; a moving flyby with suitable playback pacing is still to do.

## Play the latest Mac review

With GitHub CLI and Google Cloud CLI authenticated:

```sh
gh api repos/sunholo-data/stapledons-godot/contents/tools/install_review_build.sh --jq .content | base64 -d | bash -s -- --dev
```

The app opens directly into the current ship, at the captain’s measured eye.
**WASD** walks, **Option + drag** looks, scrolling zooms out to a labelled
third-person view,
and **E** uses the lift. Hold **I** to highlight known, visible stars; hover for a name
and click for catalogue details. Releasing **I** clears the rings; **Esc** closes
the card. **Open in map** selects the same star for planning. **M** opens navigation: select a star and hold **Commit**;
the map closes and you can watch acceleration, cruise and braking aboard ship.
**7** looks forward/up to see the star field change; pan around to compare directions.
The ship starts at rest in a live navigation session, at **4× exposure**. **J** cycles exposure; **5/6** compare frozen rest/cruise snapshots outside committed travel and
**7/8/9** look forward/side/aft. **Tab** shows all controls. **B** measures performance
on your laptop outside committed travel and displays the report location.
