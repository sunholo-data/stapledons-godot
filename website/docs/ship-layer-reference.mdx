review **one level at a time**, opening areas as they become playable.

## Next: agree the Commons plan

The approved Level 1 concept calls for sweeping terraces, open arcades, planting and
civic life. The current generic pavilion is a working test and **does not yet match
that architecture**. Review the proposed circulation/zoning before the next build;
then develop one representative arcade/plaza section with the bridge's inked finish.

## Decisions

| Settled | Direction |
|---|---|
| Ship geometry | Seven major tiers in a 200 m bubble; lower architecture follows a 95 m inner envelope for at least 5 m clearance. |
| Bridge | Keep the actual 44 m deck at +82 m and needle tip at +98 m. |
| Viewpoint | Ship and sky share one observer. Floors and roofs remain opaque. |
| Asset workflow | One measured Blender assembly, reusable painted modules and simpler distant models. |
| Release approach | Develop the Commons first, then open further levels incrementally. |
| Travel sky | Use 4× exposure for now, preserving physical inputs and directional contrast. It remains a labelled display aid. |

## Still to review

- **Commons:** agree the concept-based floor plan, then block and paint one arcade/plaza section.
- **Later tiers:** concepts and zoning remain proposals; population and life-support capacity are unresolved.
- **Laptop performance:** actual M2 Air measurement is pending. The latest Studio run is about 9.6 ms p95 at 1920×1080.
- **Travel effects:** physically supported glow/bubble effects remain future work. The current demo does not show travel glow or GR lensing.
- **Solar System flyby:** planet textures exist; a moving flyby with suitable playback pacing is still to do.

<summary>Travel sky and exposure comparisons</summary>

The demo uses a frozen simulation midpoint at **0.99c**. Turning the camera leaves the
travel heading fixed. Exposure changes the displayed sky, preserving its physical inputs;
even strong gains leave the side view subdued and can saturate forward highlights.
Geometry retains prototype lighting.

## Play the latest Mac review

With GitHub CLI and Google Cloud CLI authenticated:

```sh
gh api repos/sunholo-data/stapledons-godot/contents/tools/install_review_build.sh --jq .content | base64 -d | bash -s -- --dev
```

Open **Ship geometry demo**. **WASD** walks, **Option + drag** looks, scrolling zooms,
and **E** uses the lift. The review opens at **4× exposure**. **J** cycles exposure; **5/6** compare rest/cruise and
**7/8/9** look forward/side/aft. **Tab** shows all controls. **B** measures performance
on your laptop and displays the report location.
