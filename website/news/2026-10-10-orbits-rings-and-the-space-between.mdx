## Around Sagittarius A*

<Clip name="black_hole_orbit" label="Orbiting Sagittarius A*" controls />

The ship completes more than one orbit at **three horizon radii**. Gravitational lensing bends the background around the shadow while the native HUD shows the orbit count, clock rates and tidal load. This 20-second clip advances 1,800 seconds on the ship's clock, explicitly labelled as 90× presentation speed.

The background is the labelled **Sol sky**. A true Galactic-Centre background and a rotating black hole remain future work.

## Arriving beside Saturn

<Clip name="saturn_arrival" label="Arriving beside Saturn" controls />

Saturn and its rings grow through the final approach, followed by six seconds at a genuine, stationary arrival. The video's 16-second approach compresses 25 seconds of simulation time; earlier guided stops are omitted. The camera widens its lens near arrival to keep the close planet and rings readable.

## The space between stars

Interstellar clouds and dust are in final review in [PR #199](https://github.com/sunholo-data/stapledons-godot/pull/199). Routes cross source-based local cloud boundaries, the bubble wall responds to the medium, and individual grain impacts have an afterglow. The HUD and Archive explain the densities, assumptions and displayed impact statistics.

This work still needs its final performance, scientific and attended render checks before landing. The separate dense-cloud dataset has unresolved scientific comparisons and is deferred. The remaining first-journey audit also stays on the roadmap.
