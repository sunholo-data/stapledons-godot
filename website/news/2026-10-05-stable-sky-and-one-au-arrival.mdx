## What's new

- **One current painted ship:** seven major tiers, a bridge floor and captain shadow that meet the walking surface, faster movement, trackpad look and known-star inspection. [Integration PR](https://github.com/sunholo-data/stapledons-godot/pull/117).
- **Gradual approaches:** the guided tour uses physical 1 g acceleration with 90 seconds of braking playback. Earth, Jupiter and Saturn sit beside the bridge at their close stops. Ordinary navigation keeps its existing pacing. [Integration PR](https://github.com/sunholo-data/stapledons-godot/pull/117).
- **Stable exposure and measured stars:** one final-camera exposure update per frame prevents the white/texture and galaxy/black oscillation. Alpha Centauri A/B use measured radii and temperatures; the final leg intercepts the moving primary at 1 AU. A's disc is about 0.65° wide there. [Integration PR](https://github.com/sunholo-data/stapledons-godot/pull/117).
- **Review evidence:** the gallery and geometry reference include fresh native captures and editable Blender masters. B keeps the full benchmark locally and attempts an authenticated upload of filtered aggregate measurements. [Integration PR](https://github.com/sunholo-data/stapledons-godot/pull/117).

## Not yet

Ship lighting is still an internal lighting study; external sunlight, detailed moon surfaces and further Commons art remain in progress. Fresh M2 Air performance needs a laptop measurement. Tour stops are inertial clearance stops: gravity, thermal hazards and GR are not simulated. Stellar photospheres are uniform emitters.
