The black-hole milestone is done: you can fly to the supermassive black hole at the centre of the Galaxy, hover above it and orbit it, with general relativity in the physics and in the picture.

## Sgr A* aboard the ship

Choose "Sgr A* (black hole)" in navigation. The ship approaches from 1.3 light years, then hovers at 10, 5 and 3 Schwarzschild radii and orbits at 3. The HUD reads the simulation:

- **Clocks.** At 10 r_s, 1 ship-hour is 1.054 home hours; in orbit at 3 r_s it is 1.41. The time you spend near the hole really accumulates on both clocks.
- **Light.** It shows the shadow's size and the blueshift of incoming light.
- **Forces.** It shows the tide across the bubble, and the hover thrust, which the crew never feels.

## Built package-first

- **The maths.** `sunholo/relativity` 0.10.0 adds a geodesic integrator, cross-checked against the exact solution to 3e-13.
- **The lens tables.** They are generated on the AILANG VM and pinned.
- **The shader.** A GPU shader mirrors the CPU reference pixel for pixel.
- **The checks.** The shadow matches Synge's formula to within 0.01 px at 10, 5 and 3 r_s, and a star directly behind the hole makes an Einstein ring at the predicted angle. R1's black-hole requirements are met.

## Also

- **A light-speed loading screen.** Picking a menu option accelerates the real sky to 0.99999c, where it collapses into a point, then a white-out into the ship. Loading itself is 3 to 4 times faster.
- **A Main menu button** on the galaxy map.
- **dev.24 fixes dev.23.** The exported app was missing the lens tables, so the hole was invisible. Publishing now checks them inside the exported app.
