Clocks, speed and distance stay visible as you travel. Course plotting and journey commitments now live at the bridge consoles, while **M** opens a read-only star chart wherever you are.

## What's new

- **A quieter HUD.** Both clocks share the status strip with your speed, phase and distance. Transit, arrival and gravity cards appear when relevant and keep the central outlook clear. [PR #193](https://github.com/sunholo-data/stapledons-godot/pull/193).
- **Decisions at the consoles.** Plot and commit at the navigation station, start a guided voyage at the Voyage console, and read the physics codex at the Archive terminal. Walk there, click to walk, or use the keyboard's Walk-to list; a press-twice confirmation option accompanies the hold. [PR #195](https://github.com/sunholo-data/stapledons-godot/pull/195).
- **A chart you can open anywhere.** M shows the neighbourhood without planning, committing or pausing. Tour pause and skip controls stay instant; the decisions have no countdown. [PR #195](https://github.com/sunholo-data/stapledons-godot/pull/195).
- **Sizes for more stars.** Catalogue stars without measured radii now get estimates from their brightness, distance and temperature, labelled as inferred. Barnard's Star becomes a visible disc at its stop. Body cards also explain synchronous orbits, including when no such orbit is possible. [PR #194](https://github.com/sunholo-data/stapledons-godot/pull/194).

## Not yet

The first-journey slice still needs its complete console-based playthrough audit and remaining visual acceptance gates. Real interstellar clouds and individual dust impacts are being built on a separate branch and still await validation and render review.
