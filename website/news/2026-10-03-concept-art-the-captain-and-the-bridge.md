The ship has faces now. The captain is picked, the crew have proposed portraits, and the bridge
stands in the engine over the live sky.

{/* truncate */}

![The captain, the Longline Navigator, at 30, 50, 70 and 90](/img/concept/captain-sheet.jpg)

## What's new

- **The captain, the Longline Navigator** ([#98](https://github.com/sunholo-data/stapledons-godot/pull/98)): picked from three silhouette
  proposals, and drawn front and back at 30, 50, 70 and 90, because the game spans a 100-year
  career.
- **Crew cast proposals**: one portrait per role, next to the Medic's accepted set.
- **Bridge v1** ([#97](https://github.com/sunholo-data/stapledons-godot/pull/97), demo art): the first real interior, a disc at the top of the
  spire inside the sphere ship. The sky behind the railings is not painted; it is the game's live
  sky, through the same camera, so at 0.99c it crowds forward and turns blue.

![Bridge v1 in the engine at rest and at 0.99c](/img/concept/bridge-v1-parallax.jpg)

## Not yet

Bridge v1 is demo art. A v2 quality study is done and the final art is being made. Everything is
on the [concept art](/concept-art) page, labelled with its status.
