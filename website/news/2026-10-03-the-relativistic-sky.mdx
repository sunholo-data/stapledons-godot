The sky out of the window is now the real one: 335,157 stars over the Milky Way, exposed for a
dark-adapted human eye, with the cosmic background glowing ahead at extreme speeds.

{/* truncate */}

## What's new

- **Starfield v2** ([#67](https://github.com/sunholo-data/stapledons-godot/pull/67)): 335,157 stars from CNS5, Gaia GCNS and Hipparcos,
  instanced on the GPU, with float64 positions rebased around the ship.
- **The Milky Way** behind them: the NOIRLab panorama with the catalogue's own stars removed, so
  stars are not counted twice, Doppler shifted texel by texel.
- **A naked-eye exposure** ([#75](https://github.com/sunholo-data/stapledons-godot/pull/75), [#80](https://github.com/sunholo-data/stapledons-godot/pull/80)): stars in lux and the sky in
  cd/m², calibrated so the faintest visible star lands at V 6.9 (Crumey's model says 6.897). The
  HUD always says how it is exposing; the readability aids are off by default and labelled.
- **The forward CMB glow** ([#79](https://github.com/sunholo-data/stapledons-godot/pull/79), fixed in [#88](https://github.com/sunholo-data/stapledons-godot/pull/88)): above γ ≈ 150 the 2.7 K
  microwave background, blueshifted, shows up dead ahead; at γ 707 it is a 3,853.7 K blackbody.
