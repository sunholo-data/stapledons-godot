The journey core is complete: pick a star, commit, watch both clocks run, and arrive. Once you
commit, there is no going back.

{/* truncate */}

## What's new

- **Commit:** hold for 1.5 s. After that the simulation refuses every cancel, and says so.
- **Transit and arrival:** the phase, both clocks, the distance flown and the energy radiated,
  live, until you arrive.
- **Deterministic replay:** a 10,000-tick session replays byte for byte on the AILANG VM and the
  interpreter.
- v0.3.1 opens straight onto the galaxy map; v0.3.2 adds trackpad zoom and Retina scaling.

![Commit, and you cannot take it back](/img/gallery/map-commit.jpg)

## Not yet

The interior of the ship, and a journey you live through on board, are the next milestone (M4).
