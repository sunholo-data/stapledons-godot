## The title screen

A double-click opens a menu over the real Milky Way: **Board the ship**, the **Guided voyage**, the **Galaxy map**, **Settings** (Realistic or Auto view, text-only AI) and **Credits**, read from the repository's own citations. Esc in the ship returns to it.

## Black holes (in progress)

![Sgr A* at 5 Schwarzschild radii](https://storage.googleapis.com/stapledons-voyage-assets/refs/m3_bh/mw_r5_toward_hover.png)

M3 brings general relativity:

- **The maths is in the package first.** `sunholo/relativity` 0.10.0 adds a geodesic integrator, cross-checked against the exact solution to 3e-13.
- **The sim reports what you would feel.** Clock rates, tides across the bubble, and the thrust to hover, all at Sgr A*.
- **A GPU shader bends the sky.** It reads lens tables generated on the AILANG VM.

The shadow matches Synge's formula to within 0.01 px at 10, 5 and 3 r_s. A star directly behind the hole becomes an Einstein ring at the predicted angle; just off-axis, it splits into two images.

The playable trip is next: hover at 10, 5 and 3 r_s, then orbit, from the bridge. The HUD reads the sim, so "1 ship-hour = 1.41 home hours" in orbit at 3 r_s.
