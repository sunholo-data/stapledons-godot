The first review build: an AILANG simulation driving a Godot starfield, with relativity that is
tested rather than eyeballed.

{/* truncate */}

## What's new

- The simulation runs in AILANG as a child process, talking one line of JSON per tick with Godot
  (about 50 µs per round trip). It flies the ship at 1 g with exact rapidity kinematics.
- The starfield: 3,802 nearby stars (CNS5), each aberrated toward the bow, coloured as a
  Doppler-shifted blackbody and beamed brighter ahead.
- 27 physics reference checks, and 9 cases where the GPU shader must put a star where the CPU
  reference says, to under 0.1 px.

![The sky at 0, 0.5, 0.9 and 0.99c, looking forward, starboard and astern](/img/news/m0-contact-sheet.jpg)

## Not yet

No Milky Way behind the stars, one axis of motion, and star colours from the spectral class
only. All three were fixed in M1.
