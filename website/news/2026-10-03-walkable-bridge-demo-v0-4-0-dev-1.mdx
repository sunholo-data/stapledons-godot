## What's new

- **The walkable bridge** (M4.2, [#102](https://github.com/sunholo-data/stapledons-godot/pull/102)): the bridge disc at the top of the spire,
  composited with the live sky through the same camera. The captain walks it (**WASD**, **E** to
  use) and ages with ship time.
- **Navigation from the bridge:** a nav console opens the galaxy map (**M**), built from the real
  catalogues: 5,685 stars within 25 pc, companions at their system's distance. Commit to α
  Centauri A and watch the boost, the 0.99c cruise, the brake and the arrival.
- **The relativistic sky** behind it all: 335k real stars, aberration and Doppler colour, the
  naked-eye exposure, and the forward glow of the cosmic background at extreme speed.
- **L** opens the log and **K** the codex.

## Not yet

- The forward bubble-wall glow is off; it returns with the next simulation step.
- The standalone sky flight (`-- --voyage`) shows a mirrored sky. A fix is in progress; the view
  from the bridge is correct.
- The bridge is v1 demo art; the final art is in progress ([concept art](/concept-art)).
- Pre-alpha and unsigned; see [Try it](/docs/try-it) for install notes.
